Member overview

Members of Jet NTNU

Each group owns a part of Jet NTNU, and the groups work together wherever their systems meet.

Board

For hire＋

The Board holds responsibility for Jet NTNU’s direction, governance, and continuity.

Compressor

For hire＋

Raises inlet-air pressure and couples aerodynamic design to the engine cycle and rotating assembly.

Combustion

For hire＋

Develops stable heat release, fuel delivery, liner architecture, pressure-loss control, and temperature distribution.

Turbine

For hire＋

Extracts the work needed by the compressor while confronting the engine’s most demanding thermal and mechanical environment.

Nozzle

For hire＋

Converts the core’s remaining pressure and heat into exhaust velocity and integrates the afterburning flowpath.

Interface

For hire＋

Ensures system-level integration across all subsystems, including test rig design and shaft and bearing selection.

Mentors

For hire＋

Mentors support the technical teams with specialist experience, review, and practical guidance.

Marketing

For hire＋

Turns complex engineering into a clear public story across brand, media, recruitment, documentation, and digital experiences.

Finance & Partnership

For hire＋

Builds the financial runway, partner relationships, procurement structure, and long-term sustainability behind the engineering programme.

Avionics

For hire＋

Owns sensors, data acquisition, wiring, signal integrity, controls interfaces, and the evidence needed to understand every test.
